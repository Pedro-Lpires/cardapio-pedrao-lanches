# Fogo nas mãos do mago

Arquivo usado: `images/personagens-pedrao/ui/fogo-realista.webp` (128 × 192, PNG com transparência, aproximadamente 29 KB).

Gerado pela ferramenta integrada de imagens (habilidade imagegen), sem CLI. O original foi preservado na pasta de imagens geradas; a cópia do projeto foi reduzida com o canal alpha preservado. O CSS anima a chama e acrescenta partículas independentes que sobem acima das mãos, respeitando a preferência por movimento reduzido.

Prompt final utilizado:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: transparent compositing VFX texture for a small mobile game popup. Generate ONE isolated realistic small fire plume, a palm-sized fireball rising naturally upward from a narrow flat base. Brilliant warm yellow/cream inner core near bottom, natural orange and vivid crimson/red outer flames, wispy detailed pointed curling tongues, subtle red glow, several tiny scattered glowing orange embers just above it. Full plume entirely visible, compact vertical shape in middle 60% width and 75% height, no huge empty borders. True transparent background, feathered natural flame edges with alpha, absolutely no black background, no white background, no checkerboard baked in, no ground, no person, no hands, no objects, no text or watermark. Photographic fire texture, asymmetrical organic motion, must not look like a gradient blob, emoji or flat cartoon icon. This will be overlaid over a wizard's two open palms on a dark UI; make it readable at roughly 38px wide and 60px high.
